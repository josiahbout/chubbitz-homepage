Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

The short version: this site counts visits and clicks with Google Analytics so I can see what's working. If you become a supporter, Stripe handles your payment and I get your name and email so I can give you your perks. I don't sell your information to anyone.

This policy covers thechubbitz.com, run by The Chubbitz ("I", "me"). Questions? Email support@thechubbitz.com.

What I collect

When you visit the site. I use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to record things like:

  • which pages you view and which links and buttons you tap
  • the site or app that sent you here (for example Instagram or YouTube)
  • your approximate location (city or country), device, browser and screen size

This doesn't include your name or email. Google processes it under its own privacy policy.

I sometimes test two versions of a button or page to see which works better. While a test is running, your browser saves a small setting so you keep seeing the same version.

When you become a supporter. Payments are handled by Stripe. I receive your name, email address, billing country and which plan you chose. I never see or store your full card number.

When you verify in Discord. To unlock the supporter channels, you enter the email you paid with. I match it to your purchase and link your supporter status to your Discord account.

When you email me. I keep your message and email address so I can reply.

How I use it

  • to see which pages and links people use, and improve the site
  • to give supporters their perks and manage their plans
  • to reply to messages and help with billing questions
  • to keep records required for taxes and payments

Who else gets it

I don't sell or rent your personal information. It's only shared with the services that run the site and the supporter program:

  • Google Analytics for visit and click counts
  • Stripe for payments
  • Discord, where the supporter perks live

I may also share information if the law requires it.

Other services on the site

  • Google Fonts. Your browser loads the site's fonts from Google, which sees your IP address when it does.
  • YouTube. Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn't set tracking cookies until you press play. After that, Google's policy applies.
  • Links to other sites. Sponsors, Bash.tv, Discord and social media sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you go there.

How long I keep it

Google Analytics keeps detailed visit data for a limited time and then deletes it. Stripe keeps payment records as long as payment and tax rules require. Supporter details are kept while you're a supporter and for as long as needed for records after that.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics. Block cookies in your browser, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • See, fix or delete your information. Email support@thechubbitz.com and I'll help. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have legal rights to request this, and I'll honor them.
  • Manage or cancel your plan anytime from Manage your payment.

Kids

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Supporter plans are for adults, or for kids with a parent's permission and the parent's payment details. If you think a child has given me their information, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
