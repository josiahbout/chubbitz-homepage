      How this is counted

      Each of the four pages — /chubbash, /bashtv/chubbitz-games, /bashtv and /chubbolator — is counted on its own, then the people are de-duplicated: one person reading three of them is one person, not three. That matters for the day the old pages start forwarding to the games page, because a forwarded visit lands on two addresses and would show up twice in a plain page-view total. The headline number and the daily chart both use people, so a forward cannot inflate them; only the page-view breakdown would split one visit across two rows.
