What is it?

Chubbash is a casual mini game jam for the community, built on Bash.tv. Make one small, playable game starring a Chubbitz character. Silly is good, and you don't need to have made a game before.

  1. 1

    Make a Chubbitz

    Make a free Bash account if you haven't already. After that, you can create your character here or browse other people's characters.

  2. 2

    Use a template

    Quickly set up your game with any Chubbitz character by going here and clicking “Use as template”.

    After that, the chat bot will walk you through how to make your own game! You can also visit the FAQ for additional help.

  3. 3

    Submit by Fri, Oct 16

    Post your Bash game link in Discord with a one or two sentence pitch. Then the community votes, and the top 3 are featured in the results video.

Rules

  1. Build it on Bash.tv.
  2. Use at least one character from the Chubbolator.
  3. Start after the jam opens on Oct 9. No pre-built games.
  4. Follow the theme the community votes for.
  5. Solo entries only.
  6. Submit by Oct 16 with your Bash game link and a 1–2 sentence pitch.
  7. Keep it kind. Entries or members breaking the server rules can be removed.

Voting

The community votes in Bash's Discord, one vote per member, and the top 3 win. If there's a tie or anything needs a judgement call, Skyman will be the tie breaker.
