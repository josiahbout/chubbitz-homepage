Discord, simplified

Never used it before? This is everything you actually need to know. That's it.

  1. What it is

    A chat app — text, voice and video. You hang out in communities called servers. Free, works in a browser or an app.

  2. Servers

    The icons down the far left are the servers you've joined. Click one to open it.

    The Chub Club is one of these. Joining adds it to your list.

  3. Text channels

    Anything with a # is a text channel — one room per topic. Click it, type at the bottom, hit enter.

    Supporters get extra channels that other people can't see.

  4. Voice channels

    The ones with a speaker icon are voice rooms. Click one and you're instantly in — no calling, no ringing. Click Disconnect to leave.

  5. Screen sharing

    Once you're in a voice channel you can share your screen, and watch anyone else who's sharing theirs.

That's genuinely it.

Everything else you'll pick up as you go.
